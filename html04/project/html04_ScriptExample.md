# Lesson 04: Example Promo Scripts

Two examples of scripts are here, plus a blank template. Do not copy these, your script has to be about your own site.

Remember the limits: about 45 words, two lego characters max, say the site name at the end, and it has to time out at 17 seconds or under when you read it out loud.

---

## Example 1: Paws and Play (a pet care business)

**43 words. Two characters: OWNER and NEIGHBOR.**

| Seconds | Who | Line | What's on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | OWNER | My dog chewed the couch. Again. | Lego figure standing next to a wrecked couch, head in hands |
| 4–8 | NEIGHBOR | Paws and Play has you covered. Twenty minutes, twice a day, rain or shine. | Second figure walks in, holds up a leash |
| 8–13 | NEIGHBOR | Daily walks, drop-in visits, and photos of your dog while you're at work. | Two figures walking a brick-built dog past a tree |
| 13–15 | OWNER | Wait. Photos? | Owner turns, surprised |
| 15–17 | NEIGHBOR | Every single day. | Neighbor holds up a paper "phone" with a photo on it |
| 17–20 | OWNER | Book it at Paws and Play. | Both figures still, site name in big text across the screen |

### Shot list for Example 1

One line per picture setup. You'll shoot 40 to 60 pictures total, so each setup below is a small handful of pictures with tiny movements between them.

1. Owner beside the wrecked couch, hands moving to head
2. Neighbor enters from the left, walking in small steps
3. Neighbor raises the leash
4. Both figures walking left to right past the tree, dog between them
5. Owner turns to face the neighbor
6. Neighbor lifts the paper phone
7. Both figures still, facing forward, holding for the site name

## Example 2: Northgate (a band)

**38 words. Two characters: FAN A and FAN B.**

| Seconds | Who | Line | What's on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | FAN A | You're late. The show already started. | One figure at a door, arms crossed |
| 4–9 | FAN B | Relax. Northgate's site has the whole tour schedule. | Second figure runs up, points at a paper "screen" |
| 9–14 | FAN B | Every song, and the new album, free to listen to first. | Close shot on the paper screen with a track list drawn on it |
| 14–16 | FAN A | Free? | Figure turns toward the screen |
| 16–20 | FAN B | Northgate. Go listen. | Both figures facing forward, site name in big text |
