Kris, "The cage, with human soul and parts."

Kris's place in the prophecy is a big one, if it's Kris at all. As it seems we are the important part. As Kris (And perhaps what it means, the Vessel) is just a cage for us. Meaning perhaps we are restricted? Restricted from the cage? A cage is usually considered not a great thing to be inside of. As we know, Kris is a human, which is not what the prophecy seems to say. "A cage with human soul and parts" suggesting that Kris is not human, but just appears as one.
Freaky stuff, I know.
Is Kris really human though? As the chapter 1 info says, Kris is:
"Level 1 Human, body contains a soul."
This is a good description. But we cannot ignore what the prophecy says. It calls Kris a cage, a harsh description for sure, as they're our cage in Deltarune.
But it is metaphorical, as mentioned in Susie's section. So, Kris is a human, simply. But there is an important question. Which leads us to:

What happened to the Vessel?

What did happen to our "beautiful creation?" The easy answer is that it was simply discarded. After all, "Nobody can choose who they are in this world."
However, there is no real evidence that it is gone for sure. As many theories suggest, our vessel may be in the lake of hometown, where Kris and Noelle are going in the end of Chapter 5's Snowgrave. Will we meet our vessel? Perhaps.
But the real question... Who's to say it's our vessel? We could, yes, but just as Kris has their own agenda, what does that mean for the vessel? After all, we shaped it's mind, gave it a favorite color, gave it a favorite flavor, gave it... a... favorite... blood type...? E-either way, it has a mind of its own.
So perhaps when we meet the vessel, it will have a mind of its own. It may not want to simply be our 'human' flesh suit. It may have needs, wants, and it certainly has preferences.
But this is about the prophecy! Where is the Vessel in the prophecy? The answer is... Nowhere. Just like Noelle's snowgrave. It has no place in the prophecy, and it seems snowgrave is the true ending. Us as players looking for any way to make a choice after being told we can't. Kris fighting and trying their best... It's sad, is it not? But it's not the prophecy's end.
And if the prophecy is fulfilled, we could find that the roaring could happen.
I'll leave it here for now.

Susie, "The girl, with hope crossed on her heart."

Susie's place in the prophecy is just as big, if not bigger than Kris's place. Susie is the one actively against the way the story will "end" But whether she has the power to do that... Is debatable. Can she change the prophecy, or is she going to be the one that causes it to play out?
As it says, she has hope crossed on her heart. She has determination. Her bending of fate depends on her previous actions and whether or not they were fortold in the prophecy. But how could we know? Ralsei only said a summary of the prophecy, and we only saw pieces parts of the prophecy. But, Ralsei. He knows the prophecy. We can tell if Susie is meant to be doing what the prophecy says based on Ralsei's reactions to her actions.
Her joining Lancer? Ralsei seemed surprised, which seems evident enough to prove that Susie has the power to ignore and overpower the prophecy.
Not much more to say there; Although, I find it hard to believe there's any way Susie will be able to defy fate. As much as she tries. We will most likely see the prophecy come as told. No matter what. Our choices don't matter.

Ralsei, "The prince, alone in the deepest dark."

Ralsei's place in the prophecy seems rather minimal, aside from being the prophet who tells the summary of the prophecy, and gives each person roles. But aside from that, Ralsei is pretty irrelevant. Although, there is mystery surrounding Ralsei's light world item. But either way, there's no other way for Ralsei to do anything but be there. But he does try, and knowing the prophecy, he doesn't want it to come to fruition, despite this, Ralsei seems to get scared at the idea of not knowing what's next, so he desperately tries to get the gang back on track to the prophecy. So Ralsei may be both our best and worst ally in getting away from the prophecy.
