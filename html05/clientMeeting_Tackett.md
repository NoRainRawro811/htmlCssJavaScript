### Part 1.75: Essential Questions 

1. Basic Contact & Business Info  
Name, email, company name, industry, website (if applicable)  
2. Project Overview  
What is the goal of your website?  
Do you need a new site or a redesign?  
Who is your target audience?  
3. Content & Features  
What pages do you need? (e.g., Home, About, Contact)  
Will you provide content or need help creating it?  
Any specific functionality needed? (e.g., e-commerce, booking system)  
4. Design Preferences  
Are there websites you like/dislike? Why?  
Do you have brand guidelines or preferred colors/fonts?  
5. Budget & Timeline  
What is your estimated budget?  
When do you need the website to go live?  
These questions help uncover client needs, avoid scope creep, and create a clear project roadmap.  

## Part 2: Client Questions

1. What's the main <i>goal</i> of the website?  
2. What's your "definition" of success? Like, if the website were to be launched <i>tomorrow</i> what result would make it 100% worth mine and your time?  
3. What's your target audience? Age range, location, income, tech-savviness?  
4. What kind of design are you thinking about for your website? I request a 2-5+ color scheme, and, if you want, tell what kind of "feel" you want the website to have.  
5. What's your brand identity? Maybe give some adjectives that describe your brand?  
6. Visual dislikes? Is there anything you definitely would <i>not</i> like in your website?  
7. Do you already have a logo, brand style guide, maybe a color palette, or fonts?  
8. What features do you want? (e.g. contact page, photo galleries, etc.)  
9. Who's going to be responsible for writing the copy and providing imagery, and is it ready <i>now</i>?  
10. What's your ideal deadline? That's best for all of us?  
11. Who's going to be approving designs for the website to keep the development process running smoothly?  
12. How did your company start?   
13. Do you have any websites that you got inspiration from in terms of layout, design elements, colors, stuff like that.  
14. What, above all else, do you want your website visitors to do on the site? Book a call, fill out a form, make a purchase, etc.  
15. Who is going to be making updates to the site and where will it be published?

### Part 2.75: What <i>I</i> need 

Logo files in SVG files. (or PNG if they suck)  
Exact colors, like, the hex code.  
Fonts, what fonts they use on signs, menus, or business cards.  
Other materials they can give me, ask for business cards, flyers, or menus that'd show their 'style'  
Logo questions if they don't have a logo already:  
Should the logo have the business name, just an image, or both? Colors you want on your logo?  
Ask the client who owns the photos, or what photos they're using. And if they own the rights to use that photo.
