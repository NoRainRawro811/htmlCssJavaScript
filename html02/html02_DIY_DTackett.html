Ingredients

Pie Filling:

  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 9 eggs
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 3 tbsp corn starch
  • 1 tsp Vanilla extract
  • 2 tsp cinnamon

Pie Crust:

  • 2 (9 inch) prebaked pie shells
  • 1 egg

Whipped Cream:

  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/4 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp butterscotch liquor
  • 1 dash vanilla extract

Instructions

Pie Crust:

  1. Flour a surface
  2. Remove one out of the two pre-baked pie shells.
  3. Roll it out thinly until it is ~2-3mm thick.
  4. Using a knife or pizza cutter, slice thin strips about 5mm wide down the dough.
  5. Create a braid by using three strips, pressing them together at the top, then working your way down carefully at the top, then working the dough by laying it over itself one at a time.
  6. Take the original pie crust.
  7. Brush the rim with egg wash.
  8. Carefully place your braid on top and curve it around the pie crust.
  9. You may need to combine 2 or 3 braids total depending on how long they are to reach all the way around.
  10. Preheat your oven to 350°F

Pie Filling:

  1. Combine 1/2 cup brown sugar, 9 egg youlks, corn starch, and cinnamon in a mixing bowl
  2. Mix until combined
  3. Set aside, you won't use this until the final steps.
  4. Brown your butter in a sauce pan on medium heat
  5. You'll know it's finished when the large bubbles finish popping and small flecks of butter will be at the bottom of the pot.
  6. Remove from heat and set aside.
  7. Add milk and heavy cream in a separate pot on medium high heat until the milk begins to steam and very slightly boil on the edges.
  8. You should see a very light layer form on the top of the liquid
  9. Remove from heat.
  10. In another sauce pot, melt your remaining brown sugar and water together on medium high heat while stirring with a wisk.
  11. If you have a candy thermometer available, it will help you know when to stop heating the mixture. You want to stop heating the mixture when it reaches the soft ball stage at 235-240°F. Any higher and the mixture wull begin to firm and it won't mix into the pie correctly.
  12. Reduce the heat to low
  13. Pour in your brown butter mixture and whisk throughout.
  14. Slowly pour in your heated milk and heavy cream bit by bit, still whisking.
  15. Make sure there are no large chunks and let the ingredients dissolve on the low heat.
  16. At this point, you should have two mixtures at two different tempuratures - your original brown sugar, egg yolk, corn starch, and cinnamon mixture that is cold, and the melted butterscotch milk and heavy cream combonation that is hot.
  17. To combine these two, you will need to temper the original mixture so that it is closer to the second mixture in tempurature. (If we don't do this, the egg yolks in the first mixture will immediately scramble and ruin your filling.)
  18. Temper the two by taking the hot mixture and slowly pouring in about half of it into the cold mixture and whisking throughout.
  19. Take your cold mixture (now tempered) and pour it back into the hot mixture and continue to stir.
  20. Increase the heat to medium on the pot and keep whisking, making sure to scrape the bottom and edges so no eggs stick and scramble.
  21. Use a spatula as well to make sure you get each edge.
  22. After coninually stirring and whisking on a higher hear, your filling will begin to thicken as the aggs and cornstarch cook inside.
  23. When it is nice and thick, remove it from the heat and make sure there are no lumps left inside.
  24. Take your pie crust and pour the mixture into the middle.
  25. Smooth out the middle with a spatula.
  26. Take some tin foil and cut out a circle in the middle so that when placed on the pie, it just covers the crust and braided rim and not the pie itself.
    • This will help the pie cook more evenly since the braided crust is thinner and will cook faster.
  27. Finally, bake your pie in the oven for 25 minutes.
  28. Allow it to cool for another 30 minutes.
  29. Put it in the refrigerator for another 30 minutes for it to harden.

While that's cooling, let's prepare the butterscotch cinnamon whipped cream!

  1. Combine heavy cream, powdered sugar, cinnamon, butterscotch liquor, and vanilla extract in a stand mixer
  2. Whip on medium to high speed until the whipped cream is nice and fluffy.
    Add a dollop of whipped cream to a slice of butterscotch cinnamon pie and enjoy!
It's recommended to have previous cooking/baking knowledge before making this recipe, For some inexperienced bakers, it can prove to be very challenging.
