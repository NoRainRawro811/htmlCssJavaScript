Ultimate Party Planning Guide

Step-by-step instructions and checklists for a perfect event

Planning Your Party

Timeline

  1. 4-6 weeks in advance, select a date and venue.
  2. 3 weeks before, make and send invites.
  3. 1 week beforehand, plan the menu and purchase ingredients.
  4. Day before, Decorate and/or set up
  5. Day of, party like it's '89!

Guest Essentials

  • Comfortable seating
  • Good lighting
  • Beverage station
  • Music and/or entertainment
  • Clean restrooms

Food Preparation

  1. Prepare appetizers
    • Arrange on platters
    • Chill until serving
  2. Cook main dishes
    • Set oven tempurature
    • Monitor cooking times
  3. Prepare desserts
    • Bake ahead of time if possible
    • Keep refrigerated
  4. Set up the drink station
    • Doesn't have to be anything fancy, just a simple table or countertop will work.

Party Supplies Checklist

Party Supplies:
Plates: Paper or ceramic, 1 per guest plus extras
Cups: 2-3 per guest (for multiple drinks)
Napkins: 4-5 per guest
Utensils: Forks, spoons, knives for each guest.
